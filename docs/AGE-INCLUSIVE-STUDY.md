# Study at your own pace

The optional guided study view opens from a learning brief. It provides four
ordinary buttons: Read, Try, Check and Plan. These organize private practice;
they do not assess correctness or predict mastery. The guide is optional and
untimed. An example remains available when wanted, including during an attempt.

## Why these choices

Research supports offering recall, explanation, examples and later review, with
limits. [Karpicke and Blunt's undergraduate experiments](https://learninglab.psych.purdue.edu/downloads/2011/2011_Karpicke_Blunt_Science.pdf)
support retrieval practice under their tested conditions. [Research with children
aged 9–11](https://learninglab.psych.purdue.edu/downloads/2014/2014_Karpicke_etal_JARMAC.pdf)
found that unguided recall could be difficult and structured support mattered.
[A study including older adults](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.02997/pdf)
reported benefits in a bounded image-memory task, with uncertain generalization.
These studies do not validate this application or a single method for every age.

The [IES instructional practice guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1)
supports ingredients including spacing, examples and explanatory questions. Our
four-stage arrangement is an original design inference. A learner chooses an
optional review date; it is neither an optimal memory prediction nor a reminder.

[CAST UDL Guidelines 3.0](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/) and
[W3C cognitive accessibility guidance](https://www.w3.org/TR/coga-usable/)
support providing choices, clear content and manageable steps. These are design
frameworks, not product efficacy certificates. Pre-readers and some learners
need appropriate materials and a trusted helper; text entry is not universal.

## What remains private

The existing encrypted attempt stores the selected brief version, answer,
question, private next action and optional review date. Saving creates an attempt
revision. Nothing is automatically submitted to a teacher. Existing selected-text
sharing remains a separate preview and export action.

Reading size and spacing stay in the mounted view only. No date of birth, age
band, health condition, diagnosis, ability label, study duration or engagement
score is introduced. The guide adds no network service. Teachers should write
concrete prompts and appropriate examples rather than require a blank-page test.

## Readability and sufficient time

Native controls, named stages, keyboard access, visible focus and text wrapping
support use across devices. Reading size and optional spacing complement browser
zoom. Tests check narrow layouts and enlarged text, following the intent of
[WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/); automated checks cannot establish full
conformance or physical-device/assistive-technology compatibility.

The private room keeps its five-minute inactivity lock. After four and a half
minutes, it offers Continue studying or Lock room now. Continue can be repeated
without a limit and restores focus and the current draft. The thirty-second
warning follows the extension approach described by
[Timing Adjustable](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/timing-adjustable.html).
Manual lock stays immediate. Browser suspension may delay timers; an expired
deadline locks immediately on resumption or input. Clock rollback also locks.
Consequently the app cannot promise that a backgrounded browser will display or
announce a full warning interval. Unsaved text is lost on lock: save before
leaving. Plaintext drafts are not written to disk to avoid this tradeoff.

## Evaluation still needed

Behavioral tests check save revisions, canceled navigation, draft retention,
locking, privacy boundaries, labels, keyboard operation and reflow. They do not
measure learning outcomes. Before a real learner or school pilot, evaluate with
representative people, relevant languages, physical devices and assistive tools.
Any child-facing hosted deployment needs a separate audience, privacy, safety
and consent assessment. Avoiding an age field does not exempt an operator from
those duties; see [deployment readiness](LEGAL-READINESS.md).
